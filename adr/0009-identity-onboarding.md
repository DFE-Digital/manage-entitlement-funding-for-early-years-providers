# ADR-0009: Reuse existing identity and onboarding capabilities

* Status: accepted

## Context and Problem Statement

The service requires users to authenticate and demonstrate an appropriate relationship with a provider organisation.

Building bespoke identity and onboarding capabilities would duplicate functionality that may already exist elsewhere.

## Considered Options

* Reuse existing departmental or cross-government capabilities
* Build service-specific identity capability
* Build service-specific provider onboarding capability

## Decision Outcome

The service will prefer existing identity and onboarding capabilities before developing bespoke alternatives.

Any proposal to build custom identity or onboarding functionality should demonstrate why existing capabilities cannot meet the requirement.
