# ADR-0001: Use MADR framework for ADRs

* Status: accepted

## Context and Problem Statement

Succinctly record architecturally significant technical decisions

## Decision Drivers
- Alignment with other projects
- Familiarity among DfE teams
- Lean, not verbose

## Considered Options
Structured formats preferred, several options available

## Decision Outcome
Use [MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/)
